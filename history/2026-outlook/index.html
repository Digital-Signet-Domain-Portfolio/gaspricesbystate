Gas price forecast: the EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook for 2026 and 2027

The EIA Short-Term Energy Outlook projects a US regular gasoline retail average of $3.78 per gallon for 2026 and $3.29 for 2027, including taxes. The latest weekly survey reading is $4.07, week of August 31, 2026. A full-year projection can sit below the current week when prices ran under it earlier in the year. The Outlook is a separate EIA product from the weekly survey and is revised every month, so read it as a projection rather than an observation. [EIA · 31 Aug 2026]

Where the year stands so far

The mean of the 35 EIA weekly observations through 2026-08-31 is $3.761 per gallon. That is an observed year-to-date mean of the weekly survey, not the Outlook projection above and not an EIA annual figure, which the agency publishes only once the year closes.

Key swing factors

  • OPEC+ production decisions.
  • Permian Basin growth and US crude exports.
  • Refinery closures, with PADD 5 capacity attrition continuing.
  • Atlantic hurricane season. A major Gulf landfall typically moves the national average by 5 to 15 cents for a few weeks.

Summer driving season peak

Recent years have peaked between mid-May and early July. The quarterly projection above shows where EIA expects the seasonal high to sit, which is the honest version of a question this page used to answer with a hand-typed number.
