Three-Pump Provenance: why AAA, EIA and GasBuddy disagree

The three numbers you see quoted in the news come from three different methods, each of which lags reality differently.

  • EIA [EIA · 1 Sept 2026] surveys roughly 800 stations every Monday morning; release lands Monday afternoon ET. State series exist for the 10 largest states only. Authoritative for national and PADD averages.
  • AAA [AAA · 1 Sept 2026] aggregates daily OPIS feeds from over 60,000 stations. Updated each morning around 5 a.m. ET. Best for state and metro detail.
  • GasBuddy [GB · 1 Sept 2026] is crowdsourced - real consumers report pump prices from their app. Highest sample but a small bias toward fuel-hunters in metro areas.

When the three disagree by more than 5 cents, the EIA weekly tends to be the slowest-moving anchor and GasBuddy the fastest. We show the per-row source so you know which one is talking.
