What a federal survey can and cannot tell you about your state

Every price on this site is the EIA weekly retail price. [EIA · 31 Aug 2026] That buys you an audited, public-domain, primary figure. It also imposes a limit worth being blunt about.

  • EIA collects prices from its retail panel on Monday morning and publishes the same afternoon ET.
  • It publishes a weekly retail gasoline series for the United States, five PADDs, three PADD 1 sub-districts, West Coast less California, ten metro areas and 9 individual states: CA, CO, FL, MA, MN, NY, OH, TX, WA.
  • It publishes no weekly retail price at all for the remaining 42 jurisdictions. Their pages here carry the district figure, labelled as regional, with the series id shown.
  • Diesel is narrower still: EIA prices California and the regions, nothing else.

Sites that print a distinct number for all fifty states every day are quoting a commercial retail feed, not the federal survey. Both can be right. Only one is public record, and this is the public-record one. Source: EIA Gasoline and Diesel Fuel Update, week of August 31, 2026. Public domain.
