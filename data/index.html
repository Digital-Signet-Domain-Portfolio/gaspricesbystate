Prices are pulled from the US Energy Information Administration weekly retail gasoline and diesel survey, the AAA Daily Fuel Gauge Report and GasBuddy Fuel Insights. Per-row source stamps disclose which feed supplied each number. Independent - no station, refiner or fuel-card affiliate relationships.
