Gas sales tax and environmental fees by state

Beyond the headline state tax, EIA's July 2026 motor fuel tax table records each state's other per-gallon taxes and fees: sales and use taxes collected by the gallon, storage-tank and inspection fees, and environmental charges. These run from nothing in Connecticut, Delaware, Iowa, Ohio, Oregon and Texas to 38.65 cents in New Jersey, most of it the Petroleum Products Gross Receipts Tax. [EIA · Jul 2026]

Sales and use taxes charged by the gallon

Where a state puts sales tax on gasoline, EIA's notes give the per-gallon rate:

  • California: 2.25% state sales tax on gasoline, prepaid at 8 cents a gallon; district sales taxes can add more.
  • Illinois: prepaid sales tax of 21 cents a gallon on gasoline with under 15% ethanol.
  • Indiana: the Gasoline Use Tax, equivalent to the 7% sales tax, reset monthly; 26.5 cents a gallon from 1 June 2026.
  • Maryland: a sales-and-use-tax-equivalent component of 12.7 cents from 1 July 2026, beside a CPI component of 10.4 cents.
  • New York: state sales tax of 8 cents a gallon (8.75 cents in the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District), with local sales taxes on top; EIA's New York total does not include it.
  • Virginia: the Motor Vehicle Fuels Sales Tax, 9.6 cents a gallon on gasoline.
  • West Virginia: a consumers sales and service tax of 15.2 cents a gallon.
  • Florida: for 2026 EIA counts the excise and sales taxes together as the state tax.
  • Georgia: a prepaid local sales tax, set by the Department of Revenue from an average retail price.

Michigan stopped charging prepaid sales tax on fuel on 1 January 2026.

Taxes on gross receipts

Some states tax the value of petroleum sales rather than the gallon, and EIA's per-gallon totals leave those out: Connecticut's Petroleum Products Gross Earnings Tax (8.1% on the first sale), Delaware's Hazardous Substance Cleanup Act tax on gross receipts, and Ohio's Petroleum Activity Tax (0.65%). New Jersey's gross receipts tax is converted to a per-gallon rate each quarter, 38.6 cents on gasoline as of 30 June 2026, and is counted.

Storage-tank, inspection and environmental fees

Most states add a cent or two for underground storage tank cleanup funds and fuel inspection. Larger ones in EIA's notes include Colorado's 6 cent Road Usage Fee, Washington's 3.55 cent hazardous substance tax, Massachusetts's 3.44 cent storage tank cleanup fund delivery fee and Hawaii's 2.5 cent environmental response, energy and food security tax.

Climate programs

EIA notes that California gasoline and diesel also carry the costs of the Low Carbon Fuel Standard and cap-and-trade, but its table puts no per-gallon figure on them. Washington's Climate Commitment Act and Oregon's Clean Fuels Program do not appear in the table either. Their cost reaches the pump through the wholesale price rather than as a stated tax, so no figure for them is shown here.

Source: EIA, Federal and state motor fuels taxes, July 2026 (revised August 2026), and its notes.

Every price here is the US Energy Information Administration weekly retail gasoline and diesel survey, a public-domain federal dataset. EIA prices nine states individually and covers the rest through its districts, so each figure carries the series id and says whether it is a state or a regional reading.
