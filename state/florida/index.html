On-highway diesel for Lower Atlantic (PADD 1C) is $5.28, 32 cents below the US diesel average of $5.60. FAQ

What is the current average price of regular gas in Florida?

EIA surveyed Florida at $3.83 per gallon for regular gasoline in the week of August 31, 2026, 25 cents below the US average of $4.07. [EIA state series]

Does EIA publish a price for Florida?

Yes. Florida is one of the nine states with its own weekly retail gasoline series, EMM_EPMR_PTE_SFL_DPG.

What is Florida's state gas tax?

40.1 cents per gallon state excise, plus the 18.4 cents federal excise. [API · 31 Aug 2026]
