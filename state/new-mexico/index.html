Gulf Coast (PADD 3), EIA weekly retail
$3.62/gal regular
$5.36/gal diesel

EIA does not publish a weekly retail gasoline price for New Mexico. The federal survey prices the district New Mexico sits in, Gulf Coast (PADD 3), at $3.62 per gallon in the week of August 31, 2026, 45 cents below the US average of $4.07. Pump prices inside the district vary around that figure.

On-highway diesel for Gulf Coast (PADD 3) is $5.36, 24 cents below the US diesel average of $5.60. EIA runs the diesel survey for California and the regions only, so every other state carries its district figure here. One of the lowest excises in the country; in-state Permian crude keeps the rack cheap.

Does EIA publish a price for New Mexico?

No. EIA prices the US, the five PADDs, three PADD 1 sub-districts, West Coast less California, ten metro areas and nine states. New Mexico is not among them, so this page shows Gulf Coast (PADD 3) and labels it.

What is New Mexico's state gas tax?

17.0 cents per gallon state excise, plus the 18.4 cents federal excise. [API · 31 Aug 2026]
