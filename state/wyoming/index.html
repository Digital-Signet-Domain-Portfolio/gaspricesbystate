Rocky Mountain (PADD 4), EIA weekly retail
$4.27/gal regular
$5.55/gal diesel

EIA does not publish a weekly retail gasoline price for Wyoming. The federal survey prices the district Wyoming sits in, Rocky Mountain (PADD 4), at $4.27 per gallon in the week of August 31, 2026, 20 cents above the US average of $4.07. Pump prices inside the district vary around that figure.

On-highway diesel for Rocky Mountain (PADD 4) is $5.55, 4 cents below the US diesel average of $5.60. EIA runs the diesel survey for California and the regions only, so every other state carries its district figure here. In-state crude production is large but most refining is small-scale.

Does EIA publish a price for Wyoming?

No. EIA prices the US, the five PADDs, three PADD 1 sub-districts, West Coast less California, ten metro areas and nine states. Wyoming is not among them, so this page shows Rocky Mountain (PADD 4) and labels it.
