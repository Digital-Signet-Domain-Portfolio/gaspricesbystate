How the national average is calculated

EIA records prices from its retail outlet sample as of 8:00 a.m. on Monday and publishes the national, district and state figures around 10:00 a.m. ET on Tuesday. The national number is a volume-weighted aggregate of the district readings, not an unweighted mean of the nine state series, which is why you cannot reconstruct it by averaging the state table on this site.
