How the national average is calculated

EIA computes a PADD-weighted average from its 800-station Monday survey. AAA computes a station-volume-weighted average from a much larger daily OPIS feed (over 60,000 stations). The two normally agree within 1-2 cents per gallon; they diverge when one source captures a fast move before the other.
