EIA surveyed New York at $4.13 per gallon for regular gasoline in the week of August 31, 2026, 6 cents above the US average of $4.07.

On-highway diesel for Central Atlantic (PADD 1B) is $5.84, 24 cents above the US diesel average of $5.60. EIA runs the diesel survey for California and the regions only, so every other state carries its district figure here. Motor fuel excise, Petroleum Business Tax and a capped 4 percent sales tax make New York one of the most layered tax stacks.
