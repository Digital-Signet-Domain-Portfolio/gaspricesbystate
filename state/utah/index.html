Rocky Mountain (PADD 4), EIA weekly retail
$4.27/gal regular
$5.55/gal diesel

EIA does not publish a weekly retail gasoline price for Utah. The federal survey prices the district Utah sits in, Rocky Mountain (PADD 4), at $4.27 per gallon in the week of August 31, 2026, 20 cents above the US average of $4.07. Pump prices inside the district vary around that figure.

On-highway diesel for Rocky Mountain (PADD 4) is $5.55, 4 cents below the US diesel average of $5.60. EIA runs the diesel survey for California and the regions only, so every other state carries its district figure here. A temporary six-cent cut runs 1 July to 31 December 2026 before the rate reverts to the CPI-indexed level.

FAQ

What is the current average price of regular gas in Utah?

EIA does not publish a weekly retail gasoline price for Utah. Pump prices inside the district vary around that figure. [EIA regional: Rocky Mountain (PADD 4)]

Does EIA publish a price for Utah?

No. EIA prices the US, the five PADDs, three PADD 1 sub-districts, West Coast less California, ten metro areas and nine states. Utah is not among them, so this page shows Rocky Mountain (PADD 4) and labels it.

What is Utah's state gas tax?

31.9 cents per gallon state excise, plus the 18.4 cents federal excise. [API · 31 Aug 2026]
