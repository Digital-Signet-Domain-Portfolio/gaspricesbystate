Method

Cost per mile = price per gallon / vehicle MPG. Prices are EIA's weekly regular gasoline survey, survey week of November 23, 2026. MPG is the EPA combined city/highway rating from fueleconomy.gov; your own mileage depends on how and where you drive. To use your own figure, divide your local pump price by the MPG your car actually returns.

Every price here is the US Energy Information Administration weekly retail gasoline and diesel survey, a public-domain federal dataset. EIA prices nine states individually and covers the rest through its districts, so each figure carries the series id and says whether it is a state or a regional reading.
